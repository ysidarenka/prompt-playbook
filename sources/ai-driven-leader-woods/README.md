# The AI-Driven Leader — Geoff Woods

**Book:** Geoff Woods, *The AI-Driven Leader: Harnessing AI to Make Faster, Smarter Decisions* (2026 edition)

## Key ideas (paraphrased)

- **Treat AI as a thought partner, not a search box.** The aim is to expand and challenge your thinking, not to hand off judgment. You stay accountable for the decision.
- **CRIT™ (Context, Role, Interview, Task)** is the core prompt structure. See [CRIT](../../frameworks/crit-context-role-interview-task.md).
- **Let the AI interview you**, one question at a time, usually capped at about three questions.
- **Ask for "high-impact" and "non-obvious"** to get past generic answers.
- **The first answer is a draft.** Say what you like, what you don't, and what must change.
- **Switch personas** as the work moves along:
  - *Interviewer* pulls context out of you.
  - *Communicator* turns that context into a message or pitch.
  - *Challenger* attacks assumptions and second-order effects.
- **Simulate the stakeholder** (customer, board member, investor, approver) before the real meeting.
- **Ground the AI in real artifacts** like plans, decks, calendars, and data, and ask for sources when facts matter.

## Suggested workflow

1. Start with CRIT.
2. Let the AI interview you.
3. Ask for three prioritized, non-obvious options.
4. Refine the best one.
5. Switch to the Challenger.
6. Simulate whoever must approve or adopt it.
7. Turn the result into 30-day milestones and this week's priorities.
